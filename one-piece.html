ONE PIECE CARD GAME

ONE PIECE is a leader-based battle where DON!! resources help you play cards and power up attacks.

How to play: beginner steps

Use a ready-to-play starter deck and learn one turn at a time. Follow the card text when a card gives a special instruction.

  1. 01

    Gather the pieces

    Each player needs 1 Leader, a 50-card deck, and a 10-card DON!! deck. Deck colors must match the Leader; you may include no more than four cards with the same card number. Your Leader's printed Life value sets your Life cards.

  2. 02

    Set up

    Shuffle and set your Leader face up. Draw 5 cards; each player may redraw their opening hand once by shuffling it back and drawing 5 again. Put cards from your deck into your Life area equal to your Leader's Life value.

  3. 03

    Refresh and draw

    At the start of your turn, return DON!! attached to your Leader or Characters to your cost area, then make rested cards active. Draw a card, except the first player skips the draw on their first turn.

  4. 04

    Add DON!!

    Move 2 DON!! from the DON!! deck to your cost area each turn. The first player adds 1 on their first turn. Spend DON!! to play cards or attach it to a Leader or Character for extra power this turn.

  5. 05

    Use your Main Phase

    Play Characters, Events, or Stages by paying their costs; use effects; attach DON!!; or attack. To attack, rest an active Leader or Character and target the opponent's Leader or one of their rested Characters.

  6. 06

    Resolve the battle and win

    The defender may use Counter cards or effects. Compare the attacker’s power with the target’s power: if the attack is high enough, it succeeds. A successful attack against a Character KOs it; against a Leader, the defender adds the top Life card to their hand and may use its Trigger instead. If the Leader is hit with no Life cards remaining, the attacker wins.

Quick glossary

Know these basics

Leader

Your central card. Its color determines deck colors and its Life value sets your starting Life cards.

DON!!

Resource cards used to pay costs or boost Leader and Character power.

Active / rested

Active cards are upright. Rest a card sideways when you use it to attack or activate certain effects.

Life / Trigger

Life cards protect the Leader. A Trigger may activate when a Life card is taken, instead of adding it to your hand.

First-game tip: Plan how to use DON!!: spending it to play a card may leave less available for a power boost. Ask your opponent before resolving a Trigger.

This is a beginner overview for a standard two-player game, not a complete tournament ruling. Card text, special formats, and current official rules can change a game’s details. Ask Feasted Gaming staff if you’d like a demo deck or help learning at the table.
