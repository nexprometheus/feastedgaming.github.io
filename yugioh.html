How to play: beginner steps

Use a ready-to-play starter deck and learn one turn at a time. Follow the card text when a card gives a special instruction.

  1. 01

    Prepare your Deck

    For regular Yu-Gi-Oh! TCG, use a 40–60 card Main Deck. An Extra Deck is optional and can have up to 15 cards. Shuffle, decide who goes first, then each player draws 5 cards and starts with 8000 Life Points.

  2. 02

    Follow the turn phases

    Turns go through Draw, Standby, Main Phase 1, Battle Phase, Main Phase 2, and End Phase. The first player does not draw during their first Draw Phase and cannot have a Battle Phase on their first turn.

  3. 03

    Use your Main Phase

    You can Normal Summon or Set one monster per turn. Special Summons follow the method or effect that allows them. You may activate Spells, set Spells and Traps, and use effects when their timing and conditions allow.

  4. 04

    Battle with monsters

    During your Battle Phase, choose an Attack Position monster and declare an attack on an opponent's monster, or attack directly if they have no monsters. Compare ATK/DEF and apply the battle result. Your opponent may respond with legal card effects.

  5. 05

    Finish your turn

    Use Main Phase 2 to prepare after a Battle Phase, then resolve End Phase effects and announce that your turn is over. Some cards can be activated during your opponent's turn.

  6. 06

    Win the Duel

    You usually win by reducing your opponent's Life Points to 0. You can also win if your opponent cannot draw when required or a card effect says you win.

Quick glossary

Know these basics

Monster

A battle card with Level, ATK, DEF, and sometimes special effects.

Spell

A card that creates an effect when activated, such as changing the field or adding cards.

Trap

A card usually set face down first, then activated when its timing and conditions are met.

Summon

Put a monster on the field. Normal Summon/Set is limited to once per turn; other summons follow their own rules.

First-game tip: Learn Normal Summons and the six turn phases first. Then add Extra Deck summoning methods one at a time. The official beginner videos are a helpful next step.

This is a beginner overview for a standard two-player game, not a complete tournament ruling. Card text, special formats, and current official rules can change a game’s details. Ask Feasted Gaming staff if you’d like a demo deck or help learning at the table.
