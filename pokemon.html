POKÉMON TCG

Pokémon Trading Card Game

Pokémon is a head-to-head battle where you build a team, attach Energy, and try to take all your Prize cards.

How to play: beginner steps

Use a ready-to-play starter deck and learn one turn at a time. Follow the card text when a card gives a special instruction.

  1. 01

    Set up your play area

    Each player shuffles a 60-card deck and draws 7 cards. Put one Basic Pokémon face down in the Active Spot and up to five Basic Pokémon on your Bench. Set aside the top 6 cards as face-down Prize cards. If your opening hand has no Basic Pokémon, show it, shuffle it back, and draw a new 7-card hand.

  2. 02

    Reveal and begin

    Flip your Active and Benched Pokémon face up. Choose who goes first by coin flip or another agreed method. The first player cannot attack on their first turn.

  3. 03

    Draw a card

    Draw one card at the beginning of your turn. If you cannot draw at the start of your turn, you lose.

  4. 04

    Build your team

    You may add Basic Pokémon to your Bench, evolve Pokémon that were in play at the start of your turn, attach one Energy from your hand, play Trainer cards, use Abilities, or retreat your Active Pokémon if you can pay its cost. Follow card and turn limits.

  5. 05

    Attack, then end your turn

    Choose an attack on your Active Pokémon and check that it has the required Energy. Apply the attack's instructions and damage. Attacking ends your turn.

  6. 06

    Win the game

    Take all 6 of your Prize cards to win. You also win if your opponent has no Pokémon left in play or cannot draw a card when their turn begins.

Quick glossary

Know these basics

Active Pokémon

Your main battling Pokémon. It attacks and usually receives attacks.

Bench

The reserve row for up to five additional Pokémon.

Energy

Cards attached to Pokémon to pay for attacks and some Abilities.

Prize cards

Six face-down cards. Usually, you take one when you Knock Out an opponent's Pokémon.

First-game tip: Read the attack cost and effect before you attack. Keep damage counters by the Pokémon they belong to, and move a Knocked Out Pokémon and its attached cards to the discard pile.

This is a beginner overview for a standard two-player game, not a complete tournament ruling. Card text, special formats, and current official rules can change a game’s details. Ask Feasted Gaming staff if you’d like a demo deck or help learning at the table.
